Discord Rules

These rules apply to everyone in the SusNetwork Discord server. Please read them before posting.

Rules

  1. Follow Discord's rules

    Respect Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines at all times.

  2. No harassment

    Do not harass, bully, or target other members, in messages, voice channels, or direct messages.

  3. No insults

    Keep the conversation respectful. Insults and personal attacks are not allowed.

  4. Don't ping for nothing

    Use mentions only when they are needed. Spamming @everyone, @here, or individual members is not allowed.

  5. Don't share information while gaming

    Do not share information while you are playing a game.

  6. No self-promotion

    Do not advertise your own content, servers, or services without permission from the staff.

  7. No scam or suspicious links

    Do not share scam links, phishing pages, or other suspicious or strange links.

  8. No hate speech

    Racism, antisemitism, homophobia, and any other form of hate are strictly forbidden.
